Census Monkey Typewriter (CMT) is a collection of interactive data explorations and analyses focused on Canadian Census and demographic data. Each analysis tells a story through data visualization and narrative.

## Featured Analyses

- **15-Minute Cities**: Exploring walkability and urban form across Canadian cities
- **Language Diversity**: Mapping linguistic patterns and multilingualism
- **Housing Markets**: Analyzing housing affordability and demographic trends

## Approach

Each analysis combines:
- Rich interactive visualizations
- Narrative storytelling
- Reproducible methodology
- Open data sources
